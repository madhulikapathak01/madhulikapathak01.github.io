Madhulika Pathak is a New York-based journalist covering business, markets and technology.

She reports on the companies and investors reshaping the economy, with particular interests in AI infrastructure, capital markets and the business of technology. Her work has appeared in Forbes and other publications, and she is pursuing an M.A. in Journalism at NYU.

I report on the companies, capital and infrastructure shaping how technology enters the economy.
