Companies · markets · AI & infrastructure

Madhulika Pathak is a New York-based business and technology reporter covering companies, markets, AI and the infrastructure behind the technologies reshaping the economy. She is currently an editorial intern at Forbes, where she has worked across the Wealth, Valuations, SportsMoney and breaking news desks.

Her reporting spans breaking news, financial markets, technology, trade and enterprise reporting. Her work has appeared in Forbes, Business News Daily and CohortNews.

Madhulika previously worked as a cognitive science researcher, where she studied complex datasets and conducted behavioral research. She is now pursuing an M.A. in Journalism, Business & Economic Reporting at NYU.
